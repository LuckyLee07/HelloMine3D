Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: a single production transparent PNG sprite atlas with TWO small pixel-art HUD glyphs, for a friendly voxel crafting sandbox.
Canvas is a wide rectangle with exactly two equal square cells side by side, no divider. Left cell: a single simple fresh green leaf tilted slightly up-right, chunky stair-step outline, one pale green vein, 4 harmonious green shades. Right cell: a classic symmetrical heart silhouette in warm off-white / light-grey (no red/green), subtle 2-shade pixel bevel, to be tinted green or orange by runtime code. Both glyphs should be designed on a very coarse 16x16 pixel grid, enlarged with crisp nearest-neighbour square blocks. Minimal deliberate clusters. No tiny subpixel detail, no glossy 3D reflections, no fine noise.
Each glyph is centered in its own square cell with 20% transparent padding on all four sides. Same optical visual size. Left leaf does not cross the middle of the canvas, right heart does not cross middle. Actual transparent alpha background everywhere outside the two glyphs; no checkerboard, drop shadow or background tile. No text, labels, border, plate, frame, UI mockup, letters, numbers, sparkles, surrounding ornaments or extra icons.
The art should look natural next to chunky pixelated stone swords and block textures: polished, humble, clear at 16-24 game pixels. This is a sprite sheet meant to sample left UV half for leaf and right UV half for heart. Output a 2:1 aspect ratio PNG.
